Brand: Brand Beta (Nestora)
Document Type: Strategy Brief
Date: 2026-02-14
Department: Strategy
Owner: Helen Grant, Strategy Manager

Business Context:
Nestora has strong DTC loyalty but uneven Amazon performance. Returns and slow delivery are hurting ratings. Leadership wants Amazon to become a reliable second growth engine in 2026.

Strategic Goal:
Increase Amazon revenue share from 22% to 35% by December 2026 while protecting brand premium positioning.

Key Pillars:
1. Assortment discipline: launch only two spring scents; postpone room sprays.
2. Content quality: rebuild Amazon listings with lifestyle imagery and clearer scent descriptions.
3. Fulfillment speed: move Amazon inventory to a partner that can deliver in 2 days or less for top ZIP codes.
4. Pricing guardrails: no deep discounting that weakens the premium home fragrance image.

Risks:
A cheaper logistics partner could damage packaging quality in transit. New scents must not distract from Cedar Smoke and Soft Linen, which still drive most profit.

Outcome / Direction:
Strategy recommends fixing fulfillment and listing quality before expanding into new product formats.

Next Step:
Decision Committee should choose a logistics partner and confirm the limited spring assortment.
