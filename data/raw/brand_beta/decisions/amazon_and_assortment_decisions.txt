Brand: Brand Beta (Nestora)
Document Type: Decision Record
Date: 2026-02-25
Department: Leadership / Decision Committee
Decision Owner: Marcus Lee, Brand Director

Decision 1: Spring Assortment
Approved launch of only two spring scents: Citrus Grove and Rain Garden. Room spray development is delayed until Fall 2026.
Reasoning: Production capacity is limited. Focusing on two scents protects quality and keeps attention on core bestsellers Cedar Smoke and Soft Linen.

Decision 2: Fulfillment Partner
Approved QuickBox Logistics for Amazon fulfillment starting April 2026, despite higher cost than HarborShip.
Reasoning: Faster delivery and lower damage rates are more important than saving $1.30 per order while Amazon ratings remain under pressure.

Business Context Link:
These decisions support the Strategy goal of raising Amazon revenue share to 35% by improving speed, presentation, and assortment focus.

Outcome:
Operations will migrate Amazon inventory to QuickBox by April 10. Product will finalize spring scent formulas by March 5.

Next Step:
E-commerce will refresh Amazon listings before the QuickBox go-live date.
