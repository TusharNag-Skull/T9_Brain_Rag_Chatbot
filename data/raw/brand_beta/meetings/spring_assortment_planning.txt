Attendees:

- Owen Blake, E-commerce Manager

Business Context:
Nestora sells premium home fragrance products, mainly candles and reed diffusers. Winter bestsellers were Cedar Smoke and Soft Linen. The team planned the spring assortment and Amazon growth plan.

Discussion:
Sara proposed adding two lighter spring scents: Citrus Grove and Rain Garden. Noah warned against launching more than two new SKUs because production capacity is tight. Owen said Amazon search demand for "fresh home scent" is rising and needs better listing content plus faster shipping.

Decision / Outcome:
Approve two spring scents only. Delay the planned room spray line until Fall 2026. Owen will improve Amazon A+ content and request a logistics review to cut delivery times.

Next Step:
Share assortment plan with Strategy and ask Procurement to evaluate a faster fulfillment partner for Amazon orders.
