Brand: Brand Alpha (PureSip)
Document Type: Decision Record
Date: 2026-03-15
Department: Leadership / Decision Committee
Decision Owner: Ava Thompson, General Manager

Decision 1: Packaging Vendor
Approved GreenCan Supplies as the packaging partner for the July retail launch.
Reasoning: Fully recyclable specs meet premium grocery requirements. The $0.06 higher unit cost versus PackRight is acceptable because retailer acceptance and brand trust matter more than short-term packaging savings.

Decision 2: Influencer Budget
Approved $180,000 for a mid-tier creator campaign supporting the retail launch.
Reasoning: Mid-tier creators are more credible for gut-health education than a single celebrity. Paid retargeting will protect conversion efficiency.

Business Context Link:
These decisions support the Strategy goal of entering 120 premium grocery stores by July 2026 while keeping messaging consistent across packaging and marketing.

Outcome:
Procurement will issue a purchase order to GreenCan by March 18. Marketing will finalize creator briefs by March 20.

Next Step:
Operations will confirm production dates so inventory arrives two weeks before the first retail set date.
