Brand: Brand Alpha (PureSip)
Document Type: Strategy Brief
Date: 2026-03-05
Department: Strategy
Owner: Maya Chen, Head of Strategy

Business Context:
PureSip currently earns most revenue online. Leadership wants to enter premium grocery retail in 2026 without losing the brand's clean, science-backed image.

Strategic Goal:
Launch in 120 premium grocery stores by July 2026, starting with the Northeast and West Coast.

Key Pillars:
1. Product focus: keep the core line to three SKUs (Lemon Ginger, Berry Prebiotic, Citrus Immunity) to avoid shelf clutter.
2. Packaging: move to recyclable cans to meet retailer sustainability requirements.
3. Demand creation: run a summer influencer campaign before and during the retail launch.
4. Pricing: hold DTC prices steady; use retailer-specific promotions only in the first 60 days.

Risks:
Retail margins are lower than DTC. Inventory planning must prevent online stockouts during the launch window.

Outcome / Direction:
Strategy recommends prioritizing retail readiness over new flavor development for the next two quarters.

Next Step:
Align Decision Committee on packaging vendor choice and influencer budget so the July retail launch stays on track.
