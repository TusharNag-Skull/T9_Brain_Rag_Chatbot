Business Context:
PureSip sells ready-to-drink functional beverages focused on gut health. Sales grew 18% last quarter, mainly from direct-to-consumer channels. The team reviewed plans for a summer influencer campaign to support retail expansion.

Discussion:
Priya proposed partnering with 12 mid-tier health creators instead of one celebrity. James agreed this would feel more authentic for gut-health messaging. Lena recommended keeping 30% of the budget for paid social retargeting.

Decision / Outcome:
The team approved a $180,000 summer influencer campaign with mid-tier creators. Lena will share a media plan by March 20. James will draft creator briefs aligned with the retail launch story.

Next Step:
Coordinate with Strategy and Vendor teams so packaging and retail messaging stay consistent through the July launch.
