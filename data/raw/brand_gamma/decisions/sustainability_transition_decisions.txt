Brand: Brand Gamma (Trailora)
Document Type: Decision Record
Date: 2026-02-03
Department: Leadership / Decision Committee
Decision Owner: Claire Fontaine, Managing Director

Decision 1: Materials Supplier
Approved FiberLoop Mills as the recycled fabric supplier for the Alpine Softshell Jacket.
Reasoning: 100% certified recycled content supports a credible sustainability claim. The higher fabric cost is acceptable for a hero product meant to redefine brand perception.

Decision 2: Rollout Plan and Pricing
Approved a phased launch: 40% of Fall 2026 Alpine Softshell units will use FiberLoop fabric. Jacket retail price will increase by $15.
Reasoning: A phased rollout reduces supply risk. The price increase partly offsets material costs and signals premium eco positioning without redesigning the full line.

Business Context Link:
These decisions execute the Strategy plan to make sustainability a differentiator while protecting quality and delivery timelines discussed in the Materials Review Workshop.

Outcome:
Procurement will finalize FiberLoop contracts by February 10. Product Development will run a 200-unit quality pilot before full Fall cutting.

Next Step:
Marketing will prepare hangtag and product-page copy explaining the certified recycled materials and price change.
