Brand: Brand Gamma (Trailora)
Document Type: Meeting Notes
Date: 2026-01-28
Department: Sustainability & Product
Meeting Type: Materials Review Workshop

Attendees:
- Zoe Ramirez, Sustainability Lead
- Tom Nguyen, Product Development Manager
- Aisha Rahman, Supply Chain Manager

Business Context:
Trailora makes outdoor apparel for weekend hikers. Customers increasingly ask for recycled materials. The team reviewed whether to switch the bestselling Alpine Softshell Jacket to recycled polyester.

Discussion:
Zoe presented survey results showing 64% of recent buyers would pay slightly more for verified recycled content. Tom said a full switch could delay Spring production by six weeks if the current mill cannot supply enough recycled fabric. Aisha suggested a phased approach: convert 40% of units first, then scale if quality holds.

Decision / Outcome:
Recommend a phased materials transition for the Alpine Softshell Jacket. Ask Procurement to compare recycled fabric suppliers and bring cost and lead-time data to the Decision Committee.

Next Step:
Align with Strategy on premium pricing and communicate a clear sustainability story before the Fall 2026 catalog.
