Brand: Brand Gamma (Trailora)
Document Type: Strategy Brief
Date: 2026-01-22
Department: Strategy
Owner: Ben Carter, Head of Brand Strategy

Business Context:
Trailora competes with larger outdoor brands on style and price, but not yet on sustainability credibility. Leadership wants sustainability to become a visible differentiator in 2026 without shocking customers on price.

Strategic Goal:
Convert the Alpine Softshell Jacket to recycled materials for Fall 2026 and position Trailora as the practical eco choice for weekend hikers.

Key Pillars:
1. Phased materials shift: start with 40% recycled units, then expand if quality and delivery stay stable.
2. Pricing: raise the jacket price by $15 to partly offset higher fabric costs.
3. Proof points: use third-party recycled-content certification in product pages and hangtags.
4. Focus: do not redesign the full line yet; prove the model on the hero jacket first.

Risks:
If recycled fabric quality is inconsistent, returns may rise. If the price increase feels unexplained, conversion may drop.

Outcome / Direction:
Strategy supports a focused sustainability bet on one hero product before broader assortment changes.

Next Step:
Decision Committee should approve supplier choice, phased volume, and the $15 price increase together.
